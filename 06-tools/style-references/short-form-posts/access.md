Changing things up before the weekend. No, I’m still thinking about ACCESS, but I’m going to be a bit more of an optimist.

A lot of money got spent learning that a better app doesn't fix healthcare.

The thesis was simple: patients hate friction, build a beautiful interface, aggregate demand, win. It didn't work. Not because the apps were bad - some were great. It didn't work because the value in healthcare isn't in acquiring patients. It's in managing the complex, expensive ones over time.

But companies like Grow and Headway built real patient engagement infrastructure. And ACCESS finally gives that infrastructure somewhere to plug in.

Outcome-based payment means the money follows performance data. Suddenly the shiny front door has something valuable to offer the integrated delivery networks who've built the clinical muscle to manage complex patients.

Yes, there's still a workflow problem. But it might be smaller than we think - an eligible patient roster sync between the Part B provider and the tech-enabled care partner. Not a full integration. A handshake.

Still noodling on how CMS's proposed nationwide provider directory fits into this picture. More to come.
