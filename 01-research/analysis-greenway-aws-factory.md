# Analysis: Greenway Health & AWS "Agentic AI Factory"

## Step 1: Scouting Report (Direct Observation)
- **The Event:** Greenway Health and AWS launched the "Agentic AI Factory," an industrial-scale development platform for healthcare AI agents.
- **The Players:** Greenway Health (EHR incumbent) and AWS (Cloud/Infrastructure infrastructure).
- **The Opposition (Defense):** The high transaction costs and long deployment timelines (months) for custom medical AI.

## Step 2: Line of Scrimmage (Economic Friction)
- **Identify $I_A$ (Information Asymmetry):** Providers often don't know if a "Black Box" AI model will fit their specific clinical workflows, leading to "Lemons" fear.
- **Identify $TC$ (Transaction Costs):**
    - **Search Costs:** High. Finding specialized agents for every clinical niche is overwhelming.
    - **Consensus Costs:** Massive. IT, Legal, and Clinical heads must agree on every new agent's risk profile.
    - **Implementation Costs:** Prohibitive. Integrating agents one-by-one into an EHR is a "9-month crawl."

## Step 3: Defensive Alignment (Structural Barriers)
- **Asset Specificity:** "Agentic AI" is a Bridge. These agents are deeply integrated into FHIR data and EHR workflows.
- **The Lock-in:** By using "Greenway's Factory" on AWS, the provider is increasing asset specificity to both the EHR and the cloud provider.

## Step 4: The Huddle (Strategic Synthesis)
- **Offensive Synergy:** Greenway is using AWS's scale (Bedrock) to commoditize the *creation* of agents (industrialization).
- **The Goal:** Lowering the **Consensus and Implementation Costs** by providing a "Trusted Playbook" (The Factory) that de-risks the agent-creation process for cautious health systems.
