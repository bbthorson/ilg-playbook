OVERVIEW

Transform the way care teams work

Help physicians, nurses, radiologists, and other healthcare professionals save time and achieve more with less effort so they can focus on what matters most with Dragon Copilot.
  • Streamline documentation, surface critical information, and automate routine tasks so clinicians can focus on patients.
  • Empower physicians, nurses, radiologists, and other healthcare professionals with workflow-integrated AI—from documentation to real-time insights for better decision making.
  • Integrate seamlessly with electronic health records (EHRs) and other health IT systems to unlock new efficiencies through AI apps and agents that optimize clinical workflows.
  • Support modern healthcare demands with trusted compliance, enterprise-grade security, and high performance.
  • Support higher productivity, reduce administrative burden, enable faster decision-making, and improve patient and workforce experiences—all while driving stronger performance.
FEATURES

Do more with Dragon Copilot

Thousands of clinicians are already experiencing the Dragon difference.

For physicians

Explore the benefits of purpose-built workflows for physicians.

Streamline documentation

Ambiently capture multi-party, multilingual conversations and orders at the point of care, or through on-demand recordings any time.
Automatically convert them into specialty-specific, customizable notes—saving effort and allowing more time for patient care.

Surface information

Information is brought directly into Dragon Copilot. Ask questions and receive responses drawn from transcripts, notes, and trusted third-party medical references with clear citations. Get fast, trusted answers when you need them most, with no toggling or searching required.

Automate tasks

Go beyond note-taking with accurate coding suggestions, clinical evidence summaries, referral letters, and after-visit summaries—all without extra work. Dragon Copilot intelligently extracts relevant details, so clinicians can focus on care.

For nurses

Explore the benefits of purpose-built workflows for nurses.

Streamline documentation

Capture realtime observations ambiently, converting them into structured flowsheet documentation for quick nurse review and approval and seamless EHR transfer—reducing endofshift charting and freeing nurses to focus on patient care.

Surface information

Surface key patient data and clinical insights within workflow—eliminating the need for searches and supporting faster, more confident care. 

Automate tasks

Convert conversations into actionable outputs, such as drafting nurse notes and summaries—reducing overtime and mental fatigue.

For radiologists

Explore the benefits of purpose-built workflows for radiologists.

Streamline documentation

Accelerate report creation with integrated AI and prior report summarization—enhancing precision and reducing repetitive tasks.

Surface information

Access real-time data and AI-driven report optimization with credible answers to questions and suggested improvements to support more complete, accurate reports.

Automate tasks

Automate time-consuming tasks, such as drafting impressions and synthesizing priors, to help radiologists deliver faster, smarter reporting while lowering cognitive load.

Frequently asked questions

  • Microsoft Dragon Copilot is an AI clinical assistant built to simplify and transform the way care teams work. Designed with role-based experiences at its core, Dragon Copilot delivers the capabilities and insights each clinician needs, when they need them. Physicians, nurses, radiologists, and other healthcare professionals benefit from personalized workflows that reflect their unique tasks and environments—from encounter documentation and order capture to care coordination and imaging interpretation.
    • For physicians—available via web browser, mobile app, desktop application, and embedded within supported EHRs such as Epic.
    • For nurses—available through embedded EHR mobile solutions (such as Epic Rover) and desktop application for streamlined workflows.
    • For radiologists—available as a desktop companion integrated with PowerScribe One for optimized reporting.
  • Dragon Copilot capabilities are offered across multiple roles and regions:
    • For physicians—available in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Ireland, France, Germany, Austria, Belgium, and the Netherlands.
    • For nurses—available in the United States.
    • For radiologists—currently in preview in the United States.
