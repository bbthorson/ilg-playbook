# Research Intake — Blog: "VC-funded care enablement has hit its logical conclusion"

> **For the researcher.** This is a structured intake template for deep research that will feed a long-form blog post. Please fill in each field with source-attributed data points. Include URLs wherever possible. Where data is unavailable, write "[NOT FOUND]" rather than guessing. The piece argues that VC-funded behavioral health enablement platforms (Alma, Headway, Grow Therapy, etc.) face structural commoditization in 2026, driven by an Akerlof-style mechanism: scaling provider supply via delegated credentialing pushed the marginal cost of joining insurance toward zero, payers can't separate provider quality at the resulting scale, reimbursement falls, quality providers exit. The piece names three "exits" platforms are taking: build differentiation (Grow → Tenor), buy differentiation or sell to it (Spring → Alma), or get forced into specificity by payers (Headway → biomarker verification).
>
> The blog audience is **health tech operators**. Focus accordingly: business models, M&A, payer dynamics, provider behavior. Less focus on patient-side UX or clinical outcomes details unless they map to platform differentiation.
>
> Length and depth: be comprehensive. Better to over-research than under. This is the source material for a 2,500–3,000 word piece.
