# Publishing

**Function:** turn the ILG framework into public writing. This is where the [theory](../theory/) and lessons from [practice](../practice/) become LinkedIn posts, long-form essays, and case analyses for external audiences.

## What's in here

### [01-cases/](01-cases/)

Applied analyses of real deals and market events through the ILG lens. Each follows the [trenches-analysis protocol](02-tools/trenches-analysis-protocol.md). These are working scouting reports — raw material for posts, talks, and internal discussion.

### [02-tools/](02-tools/)

The content-production toolkit:
- [Concept map](02-tools/ilg-concept-map.md) — content architecture (what to write, for whom, in what format)
- [Voice guide](02-tools/voice-guide.md) — the "Conversational Intellectual" voice
- [AI persona](02-tools/ai-persona.md) — LLM config for deal-analysis writing
- **Generators** for long-form and short-form posts (multi-phase workflows)
- **Protocols** for context capture and trenches analysis
- **Style references** — published examples to calibrate against

## How to read this group

Typical workflow for producing a new piece:

```
ilg-concept-map.md            (decide what to write, who for)
        ↓
voice-guide.md                (calibrate voice)
        ↓
context-request-protocol.md   (pre-flight)
        ↓
{long-form | short-form} -generator.md   (multi-phase workflow)
        ↓
style-references/             (sanity-check against published examples)
```

For trenches-style case posts: the analysis output drops into [`01-cases/`](01-cases/). If a case later becomes a polished published piece, the final version goes into [`02-tools/style-references/`](02-tools/style-references/).
