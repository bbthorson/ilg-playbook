## 1\. The Philosophy

Sales is an "Infinite Game." We do not pay for signatures; we pay for **successful starts**.

- **Traditional Model:** 100% Commission on Signature. (Incentivizes "Hiding the Lemon").  
- **ILG Model:** 100% Commission on Signature (for liquidity) **subject to Clawback** (for alignment).

## 2\. The Terms

### A. The Front-Load (Liquidity)

- **Commission Rate:** \[X\]% of ACV.  
- **Payout Trigger:** Contract Signature \+ First Invoice Payment.  
- **Status:** Paid in full on next payroll cycle.

### B. The Clawback (Alignment)

- **The Trigger:** Commission is subject to a **100% Clawback** if the customer "Fails to Launch" within \[90\] days.  
- **"Failure to Launch" Definition:**  
  1. Customer exercises "Opt-Out" clause in MIP.  
  2. Usage is \< \[10\]% of licensed seats by Day 90\.  
  3. Technical Integration failed due to pre-existing incompatibility (aka "The Lemon" was hidden).

### C. The Safe Harbor (Fairness)

- **Clawback Waived:** If failure is caused by factors outside the Rep's control:  
  * M\&A / Bankruptcy of client.  
  * Key Sponsor fired/left (if "Political Map" was accurate at signing).  
  * Product Bug / Outage (Vendor failure).

### D. The NRR Bonus (The Upside)

*If the Rep does the job perfectly, they participate in the renewal.*

- **Bonus:** Rep receives \[Y\]% of the **Expansion Revenue** or **Early Renewal** if it occurs within 12 months.  
- **Requirement:** Rep must attend the QBR (Quarterly Business Review) handover to Customer Success.

## 3\. Rep Acknowledgment

"I understand that my job is not just to sell the promise, but to sell the **truth**. I accept the risk of Clawback in exchange for the upside of the NRR Bonus, aligning my wallet with the customer's success."
