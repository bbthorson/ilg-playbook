# ILG Learning Plan

**An LLM-powered training curriculum for mastering Implementation-Led Growth methodology.**

**Total time:** 10–12 hours · **Format:** Self-paced with LLM tutor facilitation · **Prerequisites:** None.

---

## How to use this curriculum

1. **Pick an LLM platform.** Claude, ChatGPT, or any conversational AI.
2. **Load the tutor instructions.** Paste [`llm-tutor-instructions.md`](./llm-tutor-instructions.md) into the system prompt of your LLM session. This configures the persona (forensic, Socratic, anti-fluff) and assessment behavior.
3. **Work through modules sequentially** — start with Module 0.
4. **Complete exercises in each module.** The LLM grades and gives feedback.
5. **Practice with [simulations](./simulations.md)** between or after modules.

**Self-paced vs. instructor-led:** Both formats use the same materials. Self-paced = rep + LLM. Instructor-led = sales manager facilitates with LLM as teaching assistant.

**New hire on-ramp:** Start with the [Plain Language Companion (Part I)](./plain-language-part-I-companion.md) before Module 1 if the math-heavy framing in the [Constitution](../../theory/01-foundation/00-ilg-constitution.md) is intimidating.

## Learning path

### [Module 0: Triage Protocol](./courses/module-0-triage-protocol.md) — 2 hours

**Goal:** Classify deals as Bridge or Toaster using the diagnostic rubric.

**Prerequisites:** None (start here).

**Key concepts:** Bridge vs. Toaster framework; market stage diagnostic (Nascent / Transitional / Mature) before cost scoring; the four-factor transaction cost rubric (integration depth, workflow change scope, consensus complexity, retention horizon); motion mapping and pilot/POC override.

**Exercises:** Score 5 sample deals; deal autopsy on a closed-lost opportunity.

**Assessment:** Correctly classify deals with evidence-based reasoning.

---

### [Module 1: Economics of Fear](./courses/module-1-economics-of-fear.md) — 3 hours

**Goal:** Diagnose the bilateral asymmetry gap and fear-based objections.

**Prerequisites:** Module 0.

**Key concepts:** Bilateral Asymmetry Gap (Δ_A) and the Fear Multiplier (F_effective = F_base × (1 + Δ_A)); Seller Ignorance (I_seller) vs. Buyer Uncertainty (I_buyer); rationality of inaction (JOLT Effect); fear vs. price objections.

**Exercises:** Diagnose 3 objections (fear vs. price); deal autopsy with asymmetry scoring; design asymmetry reduction strategy.

**Assessment:** Prescribe risk mitigation strategies (not discounting) for fear signals.

---

### [Module 2: Science of Resistance](./courses/module-2-science-of-resistance.md) — 3 hours

**Goal:** Map political dynamics and build containment strategies.

**Prerequisites:** Modules 0–1.

**Key concepts:** CFIR framework (skeptics vs. adversaries); prospective hindsight (pre-mortems); political capital mapping; containment vs. co-option.

**Exercises:** Diagnose 3 resistance scenarios; design a pre-mortem workshop; build a political capital map.

**Assessment:** Differentiate resistance types and prescribe appropriate strategies.

---

### [Module 3: Infinite Game](./courses/module-3-infinite-game.md) — 2 hours

**Goal:** Understand retention economics and incentive alignment.

**Prerequisites:** Modules 0–2.

**Key concepts:** Repeated game theory (single-shot vs. infinite); principal-agent problem; vested commission and clawback mechanics; Mutual Implementation Plans as governance structures.

**Exercises:** Calculate clawback risk/reward; design an MIP with resources and metrics; articulate the infinite-game mindset shift.

**Assessment:** Demonstrate how vested commission aligns incentives with NRR.

---

### [Simulations](./simulations.md) — ongoing (30–60 min each)

Six LLM-facilitated roleplay scenarios:
1. Red Team Workshop facilitation
2. Fear vs. Price objection handling
3. Political Mapping exercise
4. MIP Resource Negotiation
5. Deal Rescue (integrated application)
6. Clawback Dilemma (ethical decision-making)

## Assessment criteria

Three levels of understanding:

- **Foundational:** Can you explain the concept in your own words?
- **Applied:** Can you identify the concept in a real scenario?
- **Mastery:** Can you prescribe the right intervention?

**Progression gates:**
- Complete all exercises in a module before advancing.
- Reach Applied level on assessments.
- Demonstrate reasoning aligned with the [Three Axioms](../../theory/01-foundation/00-ilg-constitution.md).

**LLM feedback approach:**
- **Socratic:** probing questions, not answers.
- **Forensic:** clinical analysis, not cheerleading.
- **Anti-fluff:** demand specificity and evidence.
