# ILG Learning Curriculum

**A comprehensive, LLM-powered training program for mastering Implementation-Led Growth methodology.**

---

## Overview

This curriculum is designed to train sales reps on the ILG framework through interactive, scenario-based learning. The program progresses from foundational concepts (deal triage) through advanced skills (political mapping, governance structures).

**Total Time:** 10-12 hours  
**Format:** Self-paced with LLM tutor facilitation  
**Prerequisites:** None

---

## Getting Started

1. **Read the [README](./README.md)** for complete usage instructions
2. **Load [LLM Tutor Instructions](./LLM%20Tutor%20Instructions.md)** into your AI assistant
3. **Work through modules sequentially** (0 → 1 → 2 → 3)
4. **Practice with [Simulations](./Simulations.md)** after completing modules

## Module Structure

### [Module 0: Triage Protocol](./Module%200%20-%20Triage%20Protocol.md) (2 hours)

**Goal:** Classify deals as "Bridge" or "Toaster" using the diagnostic rubric

**Key Concepts:**
- Bridge vs. Toaster framework
- Asset specificity and transaction costs
- Diagnostic rubric (tech, org, political complexity)
- Decision matrix and override rules

**Exercises:**
- Score 5 sample deals
- Perform deal autopsy on closed-lost opportunity

**Assessment:** Correctly classify deals with evidence-based reasoning

---

### [Module 1: Economics of Fear](./Module%201%20-%20Economics%20of%20Fear.md) (3 hours)

**Goal:** Diagnose information asymmetry and fear-based objections

**Key Concepts:**
- Information asymmetry multiplier ($I_A$)
- Rationality of inaction (JOLT Effect)
- Fear vs. price objections
- Bilateral asymmetry (seller and buyer gaps)

**Exercises:**
- Diagnose 3 objections (fear vs. price)
- Perform deal autopsy with asymmetry scoring
- Design asymmetry reduction strategy

**Assessment:** Prescribe risk mitigation strategies (not discounting) for fear signals

---

### [Module 2: Science of Resistance](./Module%202%20-%20Science%20of%20Resistance.md) (3 hours)

**Goal:** Map political dynamics and build containment strategies

**Key Concepts:**
- CFIR framework (skeptics vs. adversaries)
- Prospective hindsight (pre-mortems)
- Political capital mapping
- Containment vs. co-option strategies

**Exercises:**
- Diagnose 3 resistance scenarios
- Design pre-mortem workshop
- Build political capital map

**Assessment:** Differentiate resistance types and prescribe appropriate strategies

---

### [Module 3: Infinite Game](./Module%203%20-%20Infinite%20Game.md) (2 hours)

**Goal:** Understand retention economics and incentive alignment

**Key Concepts:**
- Repeated game theory (single-shot vs. infinite game)
- Principal-agent problem
- Vested commission model (clawback mechanics)
- Mutual Implementation Plans (governance structures)

**Exercises:**
- Calculate clawback risk/reward
- Design MIP with resources and metrics
- Articulate infinite game mindset shift

**Assessment:** Demonstrate understanding of how vested commission aligns incentives with NRR
