## **Part VII: The Learning Plan (Curriculum)**

**Module 0: The Triage Protocol**

* **Concept:** The "Bridge vs. Toaster" Test.  
* **Exercise:** Review 5 leads. Classify them.

**Module 1: The Economics of Fear**

* **Concept:** The Multiplier Effect ($I\_A$).  
* **Exercise:** "The Autopsy." Review closed-lost deals. Was it Price, or was it Fear?

**Module 2: The Science of Resistance**

* **Concept:** Saboteur vs. Skeptic (CFIR).  
* **Exercise:** "The Containment Strategy." Roleplay identifying an Irrational Adversary and building a containment plan.

**Module 3: The Infinite Game**

* **Concept:** Retention Economics & Vested Comp.  
* **Exercise:** "The Clawback Calculation." Rep calculates their own risk/reward on a deal.

**Module 4: The Mutual Close**

* **Concept:** The MIP as Governance.  
* **Exercise:** "The Resource Negotiation."
