# ILG Learning Plan

**An LLM-powered training curriculum for mastering Implementation-Led Growth methodology.**

---

## Overview

This learning plan is designed for sales reps to master ILG through interactive, scenario-based training with an LLM tutor. The curriculum progresses from foundational concepts (deal triage) through advanced skills (political mapping, governance structures).

## How to Use This Learning Plan

### With an LLM Platform

1. **Choose your LLM tutor** - Use ChatGPT, Claude, or any conversational AI
2. **Load the tutor instructions** - Copy [LLM Tutor Instructions.md](./LLM%20Tutor%20Instructions.md) into your LLM session
3. **Work through modules sequentially** - Start with Module 0 and progress in order
4. **Complete exercises** - The LLM will provide feedback and assess your understanding
5. **Practice with simulations** - Use roleplay scenarios to apply concepts

### Self-Paced vs. Instructor-Led

- **Self-Paced**: Reps work independently with LLM feedback
- **Instructor-Led**: Sales managers facilitate sessions using LLM as a teaching assistant

## Learning Path

### Module 0: Triage Protocol (2 hours)
**Goal:** Classify deals as "Bridge" or "Toaster" using the diagnostic rubric

**Prerequisites:** None (start here)

**You'll Learn:**
- The Bridge vs. Toaster framework
- How to score deals on asset specificity
- When to deploy ILG vs. SLG/PLG motions

**Assessment:** Correctly classify 5 sample deals with sound reasoning

---

### Module 1: Economics of Fear (3 hours)
**Goal:** Diagnose information asymmetry and fear-based objections

**Prerequisites:** Module 0

**You'll Learn:**
- The $I_A$ multiplier and its impact on deal probability
- How to differentiate fear from price objections
- The JOLT Effect and rationality of inaction

**Assessment:** Perform deal autopsy identifying fear signals and mitigation strategies

---

### Module 2: Science of Resistance (3 hours)
**Goal:** Map political dynamics and build containment strategies

**Prerequisites:** Modules 0-1

**You'll Learn:**
- CFIR framework for implementation science
- Differentiating skeptics from adversaries
- Political capital mapping and prospective hindsight

**Assessment:** Build a containment plan for a complex stakeholder scenario

---

### Module 3: Infinite Game (2 hours)
**Goal:** Understand retention economics and incentive alignment

**Prerequisites:** Modules 0-2

**You'll Learn:**
- Repeated game theory and NRR economics
- Vested commission model and clawback mechanics
- Governance structures (Mutual Implementation Plan)

**Assessment:** Calculate personal risk/reward on a sample deal

---

### Simulations (Ongoing Practice)
**Goal:** Apply ILG concepts in realistic scenarios

**Includes:**
- Red Team Workshop facilitation
- Political mapping exercises
- Resource negotiation simulations
- Deal rescue scenarios

---

## Assessment Criteria

### Knowledge Checks
- **Foundational:** Can you explain the concept in your own words?
- **Applied:** Can you identify the concept in a real scenario?
- **Mastery:** Can you prescribe the right intervention?

### Progression Gates
- Complete all exercises in a module before advancing
- Achieve "Applied" level understanding on assessments
- Demonstrate reasoning aligned with ILG axioms

### LLM Feedback Approach
- **Socratic Method:** LLM asks probing questions rather than giving answers
- **Forensic Tone:** Clinical analysis, not cheerleading
- **Anti-Fluff:** Demand specificity and evidence-based reasoning
