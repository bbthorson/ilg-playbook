# The Bilateral Asymmetry Scorecard

Type: Internal Ops / Deal Desk  
Owner: Sales Manager & Rep  
Frequency: Weekly Forecast Call  
**Purpose:** To measure if the deal is "symmetric" enough to close.

- **Theory:** A deal only sticks when the Bilateral Asymmetry Gap ($\Delta\_A$) — composed of Seller Ignorance ($I\_{seller}$) and Buyer Uncertainty ($I\_{buyer}$) — reaches 0\.
- **Rule:** If the Delta \> 5, the deal is at high risk of "No Decision" or Churn.

## Part 1: Seller Asymmetry (What WE don't know)

*Rate from 0 (Total Ignorance) to 5 (Perfect Clarity)*

1. **The Economic Event:** Can we name the specific audit, board meeting, or financial target triggering this?  
   * \[0\] No idea.  
   * \[5\] Confirmed by CFO/Economic Buyer.  
   * **Score:** \_\_\_  
2. **The Political Casualty:** Do we know who loses power/budget if we win?  
   * \[0\] No idea.  
   * \[5\] Casualty identified and "Containment Plan" in place.  
   * **Score:** \_\_\_  
3. **The Saboteur:** Have we identified the person actively blocking us?  
   * \[0\] Everyone is "nice" (Danger).  
   * \[5\] Saboteur identified and mapped in the Blueprint.  
   * **Score:** \_\_\_

**Seller Clarity Score (Total / 15):** \_\_\_\_\_\_

## Part 2: Buyer Asymmetry (What THEY don't know)

*Rate from 0 (Total Ignorance/Hallucination) to 5 (Perfect Clarity)*

1. **The Lemon Check (Product Limits):** Does the buyer know what the product *cannot* do?  
   * \[0\] They think it's magic (Sales puffery).  
   * \[5\] They signed off on the "Gap Analysis" in the Red Team.  
   * **Score:** \_\_\_  
2. **The Work (Implementation Effort):** Do they know how hard the migration will be?  
   * \[0\] They think it's "Plug and Play."  
   * \[5\] They have resourced the "Crisis Response Team" in the MIP.  
   * **Score:** \_\_\_  
3. **The Price of Failure:** Do they know the risk of the status quo?  
   * \[0\] They think doing nothing is safe.  
   * \[5\] They have quantified the "Cost of Inaction" in dollars.  
   * **Score:** \_\_\_

**Buyer Clarity Score (Total / 15):** \_\_\_\_\_\_

## The Asymmetry Delta

*Calculate: |Seller Score \- Buyer Score|*

- **Delta \< 3 (Symmetric):** ✅ **Forecastable.** (The deal is real and honest).  
- **Delta \> 5 (Asymmetric):** ⚠️ **Risk.** (One side is blind).  
  * *If Seller Score is low:* You are flying blind. Go back to Blueprint.  
  * *If Buyer Score is low:* They are hallucinating. Go back to Red Team.
