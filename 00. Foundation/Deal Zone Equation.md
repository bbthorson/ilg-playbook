### **2\. The Deal Zone (The Parabola)**

Market transactions only occur where $TC\_{total} \< V\_{total}$ (Total Value).

* **X-Axis:** Asymmetry ($I\_A$) from $\[-1, 1\]$.  
* **The Strategy:** We invest in ILG to drive $I\_A \\to 0$. This maximizes the **Surplus Area** ($V\_{total} \- TC$), allowing us to charge a premium price.

### **3\. The Repeated Game (The NRR Imperative)**

SaaS is an "Infinite Game" governed by the Shadow of the Future.

* **Single-Shot Game:** Hiding flaws ($I\_A \= \-1$) maximizes immediate profit.  
* **Repeated Game:** Hiding flaws leads to "Tit-for-Tat" retaliation (Churn) at $T\_1$.  
* **Rule:** We must drive $I\_A \\to 0$ *before* signature ($T\_0$) to secure the renewal ($T\_2$).
