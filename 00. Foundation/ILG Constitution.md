## **Part I: The Core Economic Theory**

### **1\. The Fundamental Equation**

The probability of a sale ($P\_{sale}$) is driven by **risk minimization**, not value maximization.

$$P\_{sale} \\propto \\frac{(V\_{solution} \- V\_{status\\\_quo})}{TC\_{total}}$$  
Where **Total Transaction Cost (**$TC\_{total}$**)** is the sum of effort, magnified by the **Information Asymmetry Multiplier (**$I\_A$**)**:

$$TC\_{total} \= (TC\_{search} \+ TC\_{consensus} \+ TC\_{implementation}) \\times (1 \+ |I\_A|)$$

* $TC\_{implementation}$ **(The Work):** The deterministic cost of labor, time, and resources.  
* $|I\_A|$ **(The Fear Multiplier):** The absolute value of Information Asymmetry on a scale of 0 to 1\.
